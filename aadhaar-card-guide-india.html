Aadhaar Card Guide in India

Aadhaar Card registration, updates, download, verification and safety — explained in simple English and Hindi.

🇬🇧 English Guide 🇮🇳 हिंदी गाइड
🇬🇧 English Guide

Aadhaar is an important identity document used for many services in India. It is issued by the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) after the required enrolment process.

Important: Aadhaar-related rules and available services can change. Always check the latest information on the official UIDAI website before applying or updating your details.

What Is Aadhaar Card?

Aadhaar is a 12-digit identification number issued by UIDAI to eligible residents of India after enrolment.

Aadhaar can be used as an identity document for many services, subject to the requirements of the organisation or service provider.

Where Is Aadhaar Used?

Aadhaar may be requested for different services and verification purposes. The exact requirement depends on the service.

  • Identity verification.
  • Address-related verification where accepted.
  • Government and public-service processes.
  • Financial and other services where Aadhaar is accepted.
  • Digital identity verification services.

Aadhaar Enrolment

If a person needs Aadhaar enrolment, they should follow the current process provided by UIDAI.

1. Check the Current Process Check the official UIDAI information before visiting a centre.
2. Visit an Authorised Centre Complete the enrolment process at the appropriate centre.
3. Provide Required Information Give the information and documents requested during enrolment.
4. Complete Verification Follow the required identity and biometric enrolment process.

How to Update Aadhaar Details

Sometimes your Aadhaar information may need to be updated. The available update method depends on the type of information.

UIDAI provides online and centre-based services for different types of Aadhaar updates. Always check the current official process before starting an update.

  • Check which detail needs to be changed.
  • Check whether the update can be completed online.
  • Prepare the required supporting documents if needed.
  • Use official UIDAI services.
  • Keep the acknowledgement or reference information safely.

Downloading e-Aadhaar

Eligible users can access e-Aadhaar through official UIDAI services after completing the required verification.

Avoid downloading Aadhaar from unknown websites or links received through suspicious messages.

Aadhaar Verification

Aadhaar-related verification services may be available through official UIDAI channels. Use the official service whenever you need to verify Aadhaar information.

How to Keep Aadhaar Safe

  • Do not share Aadhaar details unnecessarily.
  • Do not share OTPs with anyone.
  • Avoid suspicious Aadhaar update messages.
  • Use official UIDAI websites and services.
  • Be careful with photocopies of your documents.
  • Do not enter Aadhaar information on unknown websites.
Never share your OTP or confidential authentication information with anyone. Fraudsters may pretend to be government officials, bank employees or customer-care representatives.

Common Aadhaar Scams

  • Fake Aadhaar update messages.
  • Fake customer-care numbers.
  • Fraudulent websites asking for Aadhaar details.
  • Requests for OTP or authentication details.
  • Fake KYC or account-blocking messages.
Safety Tip: If a message asks you to click a link immediately to prevent your Aadhaar from being blocked, do not panic. Verify the information through official UIDAI channels.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aadhaar Card?

Aadhaar is a 12-digit identification number issued by UIDAI to eligible residents after the required enrolment process.

Can Aadhaar details be updated?

Yes. Depending on the type of information, available UIDAI online services or an Aadhaar update centre may be used.

Can I download e-Aadhaar online?

Eligible users can use official UIDAI services to access and download e-Aadhaar after the required verification.

Is it safe to share Aadhaar everywhere?

You should avoid sharing Aadhaar information unnecessarily. Always understand why the information is being requested and prefer trusted and official channels.

आधार बनवाने की प्रक्रिया

अगर आपको आधार बनवाना है, तो सबसे पहले UIDAI की current information check करें और उसके अनुसार enrolment process पूरा करें।

1. पहले जानकारी Check करें

Aadhaar enrolment से पहले official UIDAI information देखें।

2. Authorised Centre जाएँ

जरूरत के अनुसार authorised Aadhaar centre पर जाएँ।

3. जरूरी जानकारी दें

Enrolment के दौरान मांगी गई जानकारी और documents दें।

4. Verification पूरा करें

जरूरी identity और biometric process पूरा करें।

आधार में जानकारी कैसे Update करें?

अगर आपके आधार में कोई जानकारी बदल गई है या गलत है, तो उसे update करने की जरूरत हो सकती है।

कौन-सी जानकारी online update हो सकती है और किसके लिए centre जाना पड़ सकता है, यह current UIDAI process पर depend करता है।

  • सबसे पहले देखें कि कौन-सी जानकारी बदलनी है।
  • Check करें कि वह update online हो सकता है या नहीं।
  • जरूरत हो तो supporting documents तैयार रखें।
  • सिर्फ official UIDAI services का इस्तेमाल करें।
  • Update की acknowledgement या reference details संभालकर रखें।

e-Aadhaar कैसे Download करें?

जरूरी verification पूरा होने के बाद eligible users official UIDAI services के जरिए अपना e-Aadhaar access और download कर सकते हैं।

Aadhaar download करने के लिए किसी unknown website या suspicious message में दिए गए link का इस्तेमाल न करें।

आधार Verification

आधार से जुड़ी verification services official UIDAI channels के जरिए उपलब्ध हो सकती हैं। Verification के लिए हमेशा official service को प्राथमिकता दें।

आधार को सुरक्षित कैसे रखें?
