How to Check a Government Scheme

Follow these simple steps before applying for any scheme.

1

Find the Scheme

Identify the government scheme or service that matches your needs.

2

Check Eligibility

Carefully check age, income, location and other eligibility requirements.

3

Verify Official Details

Confirm the latest information through the relevant official government portal.

4

Apply Safely

Use the correct official application process and keep your receipt or application number.

Use Official Government Sources

  • Always verify important information on official government websites.
  • Check eligibility requirements before applying for a scheme.
  • Never share OTPs, passwords or sensitive information with strangers.
  • Be careful with websites promising guaranteed government benefits.
  • Keep application numbers, receipts and important documents safely stored.
  • Check the latest rules, fees and requirements before applying.
Important: Government schemes, eligibility rules, fees and application procedures can change. IndiaGuide is an informational website and is not a government department. Always confirm current information through the relevant official government department or portal before applying.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about government services in India.

What government services are available in India?

Government services cover many areas including identity documents, certificates, healthcare, education, taxation, pensions, welfare schemes and digital citizen services.

Where should I verify government scheme information?

Important information should always be checked through the relevant official government department or government portal.

Can government rules and requirements change?

Yes. Eligibility requirements, fees, documents and application procedures can change. Always check the latest information before submitting an application.

Is IndiaGuide an official government website?

No. IndiaGuide is an independent informational website. Users should verify important information through official government sources before taking action.
