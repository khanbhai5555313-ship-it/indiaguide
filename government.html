Use Official Government Sources

  • Always verify important information on official government websites.
  • Check eligibility requirements before applying for a scheme.
  • Never share OTPs, passwords or sensitive information with strangers.
  • Be careful with websites promising guaranteed government benefits.
  • Keep application numbers and receipts safely stored.
  • Check the latest rules before submitting an application.
Important: Government schemes, eligibility rules, fees and application procedures can change. IndiaGuide is an informational website. Always confirm current details through the relevant official government department or portal before applying.
