How to Open a Bank Account in India

A simple step-by-step guide covering bank account types, KYC documents, eligibility, account opening and useful banking tips.

📅 Published: September 2026 👤 IndiaGuide 🏷️ Banking

Opening a bank account is an important part of managing money in India. A bank account can help you save money, receive salary, make payments, transfer funds and use other banking services.

In this guide, we explain the basic process of opening a bank account in simple language, including common account types, KYC requirements and important things to check before applying.

1

Types of Bank Accounts in India

Banks offer different types of accounts depending on how the customer plans to use the account. Some common types include:

🐷

Savings Account

  • Commonly used by individuals
  • Suitable for saving money
  • Useful for everyday banking
💳

Current Account

  • Commonly used for business banking
  • Designed for frequent transactions
  • Terms vary by bank
👨‍💼

Salary Account

  • Often offered to salaried employees
  • Salary can be credited directly
  • Features vary by employer and bank
2

Documents Required for KYC

Banks need customer information and KYC documentation before opening an account. The exact documents required can vary depending on the bank, account type and customer.

  • Identity and address proof as accepted by the bank
  • PAN or applicable tax identification details
  • Photograph or other information requested by the bank
  • Mobile number and other contact details
  • Additional documents where required
Quick Tip: Before visiting a branch, check the bank's official website or contact the bank to confirm the latest KYC requirements. Requirements can vary between banks.
3

Who Can Open a Bank Account?

Eligibility depends on the type of account and the bank's rules. In general, customers need to provide the information and KYC documentation required by the bank.

  • Meet the bank's eligibility requirements
  • Provide required KYC information
  • Provide valid documents accepted by the bank
  • Complete the bank's account-opening process
4

5

Online vs Branch Account Opening

Many banks provide digital account-opening options, while traditional branch-based account opening is also available. Which option is suitable depends on the bank and the account you want to open.

Online Account Opening

  • Can be convenient from home
  • Some banks offer digital verification
  • Documents may be submitted digitally
  • Availability depends on the bank and account

Branch Account Opening

  • Useful if you prefer in-person assistance
  • Bank staff can explain account features
  • Physical documents may be checked at the branch
6

Check Minimum Balance and Bank Charges

Before opening an account, carefully check the bank's current terms and charges. Some accounts may have specific balance requirements, while others may have different conditions.

Important: Do not assume that every bank account has the same minimum balance, ATM limits, transaction charges or other fees. Always check the current schedule of charges and account terms provided by the bank.

Banking Safety Tips

  • Never share your ATM or debit-card PIN.
  • Never share an OTP with an unknown person.
  • Do not click suspicious banking links received through messages.
  • Use the official banking app or official bank website.
  • Check the recipient details before making a digital payment.
  • Contact your bank immediately if you notice suspicious activity.
7

Frequently Asked Questions

Which documents are needed to open a bank account in India?

Banks generally require KYC information and officially valid documents. The exact requirements can vary by bank, account type and customer circumstances.

Can I open a bank account online in India?

Many banks provide online account-opening facilities. The process and verification method depend on the bank and the account being opened.

What is a savings account?

A savings account is a deposit account commonly used by individuals for saving money and accessing everyday banking services.

Can a minor have a bank account in India?

Banks can offer deposit accounts for minors subject to applicable rules and the bank's procedures. Operating conditions can depend on age and the bank's policy.

Important: Banking rules, KYC requirements, charges and account features can change and may differ between banks. This article is for general informational purposes. Always verify important information with the relevant bank or official authority before taking financial action.
