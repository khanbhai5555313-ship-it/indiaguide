KYC Documents Required for Bank Account in India

Understand KYC, identity proof, address proof, PAN requirements and the basic process of completing KYC for banking services.

📅 Published: September 2026 👤 IndiaGuide 🏷️ Banking & KYC

KYC is an important part of banking in India. Banks use KYC procedures to establish and verify customer information before providing many banking services.

The exact documents and verification process can vary depending on the bank, account type and customer's situation. This guide explains the common requirements in simple language.

1

What Is KYC in Banking?

KYC stands for Know Your Customer. It refers to procedures used by banks and other regulated financial institutions to establish customer identity and collect required information.

In simple words: KYC helps a bank verify who the customer is and maintain the information required for the customer's banking relationship.
2

Common KYC Documents

Depending on the bank and circumstances, customers may need documents or information used for identity, address and other applicable verification requirements.

🪪

Identity Proof

Banks may accept officially valid documents for establishing identity according to applicable KYC requirements.

🏠

Address Information

Address details or accepted proof may be required depending on the account and customer's circumstances.

💳

PAN Details

PAN or applicable tax identification information may be required for relevant banking services.

📷

Photograph

A photograph or digital image may be requested depending on the bank's account-opening process.

📱

Mobile Number

Banks may collect mobile and contact information for communication and banking services.

📄

Additional Documents

Additional information may be requested depending on the account type or customer's circumstances.

Keep Your Documents Ready

Before opening an account or completing a KYC process, check the bank's current requirements.

  • Check the bank's official requirements.
  • Keep your documents readable and up to date.
  • Make sure the information matches your application.
  • Follow the bank's verification instructions.
  • Keep copies of important submitted documents where appropriate.
3

4

Can KYC Be Completed Online?

Some banks provide digital account-opening and KYC facilities. The exact process can depend on the bank, account type and customer's circumstances.

  • Use only the bank's official website or app.
  • Follow the bank's verification instructions.
  • Do not share OTPs or passwords with other people.
  • Check that you are using the genuine banking service.
Safety reminder: Never send your banking password, ATM PIN or OTP to someone claiming to help with KYC. If you receive a suspicious message, contact the bank through an official channel.
5

How to Update KYC

If your bank asks you to update KYC, follow the instructions provided by the bank through its official channels.

  • Check the bank's official KYC update instructions.
  • Provide the information or documents requested.
  • Complete any required verification.
  • Keep confirmation or acknowledgement where provided.
Remember: KYC update procedures can differ between banks and may change over time. Always confirm the current process with your bank.
6

Common KYC Mistakes to Avoid

  • Submitting incorrect or outdated information.
  • Ignoring a bank's request for KYC information.
  • Using unofficial websites or suspicious links.
  • Sharing OTPs, passwords or PINs with strangers.
  • Not checking the bank's latest requirements.

Important Banking Safety Tips

  • Never share your ATM or debit-card PIN.
  • Never share an OTP with an unknown person.
  • Do not click suspicious links claiming to update KYC.
  • Use the official website or mobile app of your bank.
  • Do not give your banking password to anyone.
  • Contact your bank immediately if you notice suspicious activity.
7

Frequently Asked Questions

What is KYC in banking?

KYC means Know Your Customer. Banks use KYC procedures to verify customer identity and collect required information.

Which documents are required for bank KYC?

Requirements depend on the bank, account type and customer circumstances. Banks generally require applicable KYC information and accepted documents.

Is PAN required for a bank account?

PAN or applicable tax identification information may be required depending on the account and applicable rules. Always verify the current requirement with your bank.

Can KYC be updated online?

Some banks provide digital KYC or KYC update facilities. Availability depends on the bank and the customer's situation.

Important: KYC rules, accepted documents and verification procedures can change and may differ between banks and customer circumstances. This article is for general informational purposes. Always verify current requirements with the relevant bank or official authority before submitting documents or taking financial action.
