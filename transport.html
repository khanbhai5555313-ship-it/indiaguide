Travel Safely in India

  • Plan your route before starting a long journey.
  • Keep tickets, identification and important documents safe.
  • Use official transport websites or trusted booking services.
  • Keep your luggage secure, especially in crowded places.
  • Follow local traffic and transport rules.
  • Allow extra travel time for delays and busy periods.
Travel Safety Tip: Transport schedules, fares, routes and rules can change. Always check the latest information through the relevant official transport authority or service provider before travelling.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about transportation in India.

What are the main types of transport in India?

Common transport options include trains, buses, domestic flights, metro systems, taxis, auto rickshaws, cars, motorcycles and scooters.

Which transport is best for long-distance travel in India?

The best option depends on distance, budget, schedule and personal preference. Trains, buses and domestic flights are common choices.

What should I carry when travelling in India?

Keep your ticket or booking details, identification, essential documents, phone, payment method and important personal items secure.

How can I travel safely in India?

Plan your route, use trusted transport services, keep belongings secure, follow traffic and transport rules and check the latest official information.
