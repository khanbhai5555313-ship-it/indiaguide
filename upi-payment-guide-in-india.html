UPI Payment Guide in India

Learn how UPI works, how to create a UPI ID, make payments using QR codes, use your UPI PIN, handle failed transactions and protect yourself from online payment fraud.

What Is UPI?

UPI stands for Unified Payments Interface. It allows people to send and receive money electronically through supported UPI apps using a linked bank account.

Instead of entering bank account details for every payment, users can generally make payments using a UPI ID, mobile number, QR code or other supported payment options.

Simple example: You buy something from a shop, scan the shop's QR code, check the recipient and amount, and authorize the payment using your UPI PIN.

What Do You Need to Use UPI?

Generally, you need:

  • A bank account that supports UPI.
  • A mobile number linked with your bank account.
  • A supported UPI application.
  • A debit card or other setup details where required by the app/bank.
  • A UPI PIN for authorizing transactions.
Important: Exact setup requirements can vary depending on your bank and the UPI app you use.

How to Create a UPI ID

Step 1 — Install a UPI App Choose a supported UPI application from your phone's official app store.
Step 2 — Verify Your Mobile Number Use the mobile number linked with your bank account when required.
Step 3 — Select Your Bank Choose your bank and follow the app's account-linking process.
Step 4 — Set Up UPI Follow the instructions to create or set your UPI PIN if required.

What Is a UPI ID?

A UPI ID, also called a Virtual Payment Address in some contexts, is an identifier used for UPI transactions.

It can allow another person to send money to you without requiring you to share your full bank account number.

Remember: Your UPI ID and UPI PIN are not the same thing. Never share your UPI PIN with anyone.

How to Pay Using UPI

  1. Open your UPI application.
  2. Select the payment option.
  3. Choose the recipient or scan the QR code.
  4. Enter the amount carefully.
  5. Check the recipient name and transaction details.
  6. Authorize the payment using your UPI PIN.
  7. Wait for the transaction confirmation.
Before pressing Pay: Always check the recipient's name, amount and payment details.

How to Pay by Scanning a QR Code

QR payments are commonly used at shops, restaurants, service counters and other businesses.

  1. Open your UPI app.
  2. Tap the Scan QR option.
  3. Scan the merchant's QR code.
  4. Check the displayed recipient details.
  5. Enter the correct amount if necessary.
  6. Confirm the payment.
  7. Enter your UPI PIN only when you are intentionally making the payment.
Fraud warning: Do not scan unknown QR codes or approve unexpected payment requests just because someone tells you that scanning the code will "receive" money.

What Is a UPI PIN?

A UPI PIN is a confidential authentication number used to authorize UPI transactions.

Never Share Your UPI PIN

Bank employees, UPI support representatives and genuine customer-care staff should not ask you to reveal your confidential UPI PIN.

Never share: UPI PIN, OTP, card PIN, CVV or other confidential banking authentication information with strangers.

UPI Payment Failed or Pending – What to Do?

Sometimes a UPI transaction may show as failed, pending or otherwise incomplete because of connectivity, bank, app or system issues.

If Money Was Not Debited

  • Check the transaction status.
  • Wait a short while and try again if appropriate.
  • Make sure your internet connection is working.

If Money Was Debited

  • Do not immediately repeat the payment.
  • Check your bank account and UPI transaction history.
  • Save the transaction/reference number.
  • Follow the complaint or support process provided by your UPI app or bank.
Keep screenshots and transaction details when reporting a disputed or failed payment.

Common UPI Scams to Watch Out For

1. Fake Customer Care

Scammers may pretend to be customer-care representatives and ask for OTPs, PINs or remote access to your phone.

2. Fake Refund

Someone may claim that they are sending you a refund and ask you to enter your UPI PIN or approve a payment request.

3. Unknown QR Code

Be careful when someone asks you to scan a QR code without clearly explaining the transaction.

4. Screen-Sharing or Remote-Access Apps

Never install unknown applications or give strangers control of your phone just to resolve a payment issue.

Important UPI Safety Tips

  • Never share your UPI PIN.
  • Never share OTPs with strangers.
  • Verify the recipient before making a payment.
  • Do not approve unknown payment requests.
  • Do not trust random customer-care numbers found online.
  • Keep your phone and UPI application updated.
  • Use your bank's official contact channels for complaints.
  • Check your bank account regularly for unusual transactions.

UPI Payment: Sending vs Receiving Money

Sending Money You normally select the recipient, enter the amount and authorize the transaction using your UPI PIN.
Receiving Money You may provide your UPI ID or other supported payment details to the sender.
Important: Receiving money should not require you to reveal your UPI PIN. Be suspicious of anyone asking you to enter your PIN to receive money.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is UPI? UPI is a digital payment system that allows users to send and receive money through supported UPI applications and linked bank accounts.
What is a UPI ID? A UPI ID is a payment identifier that can be used to send or receive money through UPI.
Should I share my UPI PIN? No. Your UPI PIN is confidential and should never be shared.
What should I do if my UPI payment fails? Check the transaction status and your bank account. If money was debited, keep the transaction reference number and contact your bank or UPI app through its official support process.
Can scanning a QR code automatically receive money? Be careful. Scanning a QR code can be part of making a payment. Always understand what transaction you are approving before entering your UPI PIN.
