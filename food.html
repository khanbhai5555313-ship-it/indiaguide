Discover the Taste of India

  • Every region of India has its own food traditions.
  • Spices and cooking methods vary widely across states.
  • Street food is an important part of Indian food culture.
  • Indian cuisine includes vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes.
  • Many festivals and celebrations have special traditional foods.
  • Trying local food while travelling can help you experience regional culture.
Food Safety Tip: When eating street food or travelling, choose clean and hygienic places. Food quality and preparation standards can vary from one place to another.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Indian food and cuisine.

What are some famous Indian foods?

Popular Indian foods include biryani, dosa, idli, butter chicken, paneer dishes, samosa, chaat and pav bhaji.

Which region of India is famous for biryani?

Biryani is popular throughout India. Hyderabad, Lucknow and Kolkata are among the places particularly known for distinctive regional styles.

What is popular South Indian food?

Dosa, idli, vada, sambar, uttapam and many rice-based dishes are popular examples of South Indian cuisine.

Is Indian food mostly vegetarian?

India has both vegetarian and non-vegetarian cuisines. Food preferences vary considerably by region, culture and individual choice.
