Types of Bank Accounts in India

Understand savings, current, salary, joint and other common bank account types and learn which type may suit different everyday needs.

Why Are There Different Types of Bank Accounts?

Banks offer different account types because customers have different financial needs.

Someone saving money for everyday expenses may need a different account from a business that makes many transactions. Similarly, salary earners, families and young customers may have different account options.

Important: Account names, features, eligibility, minimum balance rules and charges can vary between banks. Always check the current terms of the bank before opening an account.

Common Types of Bank Accounts in India

🏦 Savings Account

Commonly used by individuals for saving money and managing everyday personal banking needs.

💼 Current Account

Generally designed for businesses and customers who need frequent transactions and business banking services.

💰 Salary Account

Usually used for receiving salary payments from an employer under an arrangement with the bank.

👨‍👩‍👧 Joint Account

An account that can be operated by more than one person, subject to the bank's account rules and operating instructions.

👦 Minor Account

Accounts designed for children or minors may be available subject to applicable rules, eligibility and bank policy.

🪙 Zero-Balance Options

Some account products may not require a conventional minimum balance. Conditions and features depend on the specific account.

1. Savings Account

A savings account is one of the most common bank accounts used by individuals.

Common Uses

  • Keeping personal savings in a bank.
  • Receiving money and payments.
  • Using ATM or debit-card services where available.
  • Using online and mobile banking.
  • Making digital payments such as UPI where supported.
Suitable for: People who mainly need a personal account for saving money and everyday transactions.

2. Current Account

A current account is generally intended for businesses, firms and customers who may need frequent banking transactions.

Common Uses

  • Business transactions.
  • Receiving and making business payments.
  • Managing regular commercial transactions.
  • Using business-oriented banking services where available.
Remember: Current account features, charges and transaction conditions vary by bank and account type.

3. Salary Account

A salary account is generally used to receive salary payments from an employer.

An employer may have a relationship with a particular bank, allowing employees to receive their salary through accounts opened under that arrangement.

Possible Features

  • Salary credit through the employer.
  • Debit-card services.
  • Mobile and internet banking.
  • Digital payment facilities.
Important: Features and balance requirements depend on the bank, employer arrangement and specific account product.

4. Joint Bank Account

A joint account is an account held by two or more individuals, subject to the bank's rules.

Who May Use One?

  • Spouses.
  • Family members.
  • Business partners where permitted.
  • Other eligible individuals.

Banks may offer different operating instructions for joint accounts. Before opening one, understand who can operate the account and under what conditions.

5. Bank Account for Minors

Banks may provide account products for children and minors, subject to applicable rules and the bank's eligibility conditions.

Depending on the customer's age and the bank's policy, a parent or guardian may have a role in opening or operating the account.

Check first: Age limits, documents, operating rules and account features can differ between banks.

6. Zero-Balance Bank Account Options

Some bank account products are designed without a conventional minimum-balance requirement, subject to the product's specific terms and conditions.

"Zero balance" does not automatically mean that every banking service is free. Other charges, limits or conditions may still apply.

Tip: Always read the current account terms and schedule of charges before opening an account.

How to Choose the Right Bank Account

Before opening an account, think about how you will use it.

  1. Decide whether the account is for personal or business use.
  2. Check whether you need salary-credit facilities.
  3. Compare minimum-balance requirements.
  4. Check applicable fees and charges.
  5. Compare ATM, debit-card and digital-banking facilities.
  6. Check transaction limits and other important conditions.
  7. Read the bank's current terms before applying.

Documents Usually Needed to Open a Bank Account

The exact documents required depend on the bank, account type and customer circumstances.

Banks may ask for identity, address and other required information as part of their account-opening and KYC process.

For more information: See our detailed KYC Documents Guide for a general overview.

Can You Open a Bank Account Online?

Many banks provide online or digital account-opening options for eligible customers.

The exact process may include mobile verification, identity verification and other steps required by the bank.

Safety tip: Start account opening only through the bank's official website, official application or an authorized branch/channel.

Frequently Asked Questions
