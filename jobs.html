How to Start Your Job Search

Follow these simple steps to build a stronger career search.

1

Choose a Career

Think about your interests, education, abilities and the type of work you want.

2

Build Skills

Improve relevant technical, communication and practical skills.

3

Create a Resume

Prepare a simple professional resume highlighting your relevant qualifications.

4

Apply Carefully

Use trusted recruitment sources and verify job details before applying.

Build Your Career Step by Step

  • Choose a career direction based on your interests and abilities.
  • Build useful skills through education and practical experience.
  • Create a clear and professional resume.
  • Prepare properly before attending interviews.
  • Use trusted job portals and official recruitment websites.
  • Keep learning as your career develops.
Job Safety Tip: Be careful with online job scams. Never pay money simply because someone promises a guaranteed job. Never share OTPs, passwords, PINs or sensitive banking information with unknown people. Always verify the employer and recruitment source before proceeding.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about jobs and careers in India.

How can I find jobs in India?

You can explore official recruitment websites, trusted job portals, company career pages and other reliable sources when searching for jobs in India.

How can I prepare for a job interview?

Understand the job role, review your resume, practise common interview questions and improve your communication and professional presentation.

How can I avoid job scams?

Be careful with job offers demanding money upfront or asking for OTPs, passwords or sensitive banking information. Verify the employer and recruitment source before proceeding.

What should a good resume include?

A resume should clearly present relevant education, skills, experience, achievements and contact information in a simple, professional format.
