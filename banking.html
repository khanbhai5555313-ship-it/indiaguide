Stay Safe While Banking

  • Never share your ATM PIN with anyone.
  • Never share an OTP with unknown people.
  • Do not click suspicious banking links.
  • Use official banking apps and websites.
  • Contact your bank immediately if you notice suspicious activity.
  • Keep your banking documents and passwords secure.
Important: Banking rules, charges and document requirements can vary between banks and can change over time. Always verify important information with the relevant bank or official authority before taking financial action.
