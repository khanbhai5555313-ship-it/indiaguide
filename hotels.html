Things to Check Before Booking

  • Check the hotel's location and distance from your destination.
  • Read recent guest reviews before making a decision.
  • Compare the total price and check for additional charges.
  • Review cancellation and refund policies.
  • Check available facilities and room details.
  • Use trusted booking platforms or the hotel's official website.
Booking Safety Tip: Be careful with fake hotel offers and suspicious payment requests. Verify the property and booking details before sending money or sharing personal information.
