Keep Your Documents Safe

  • Keep original documents in a secure place.
  • Maintain safe digital copies of important documents.
  • Do not share sensitive document details unnecessarily.
  • Check official websites before submitting applications.
  • Be careful with fake document-service websites and scams.
  • Verify requirements before visiting an office.
Important: Document requirements and government procedures can vary and may change. Always confirm current requirements through the relevant official government authority before applying.
