🇬🇧 Driving Licence Guide – English

What is a Driving Licence?

A Driving Licence (DL) is an official document that authorises an eligible person to drive certain types of motor vehicles according to the applicable rules.

In India, driving licence services are handled through the applicable transport authorities and the authorised government system.

Simple meaning: A Driving Licence shows that you are legally authorised to drive the vehicle category mentioned on your licence.

Why is a Driving Licence important?

  • It is required for legally driving applicable motor vehicles.
  • It identifies the licensed driver.
  • It contains information about authorised vehicle categories.
  • It may be required during traffic checks.
  • It can also serve as an important identity document.

Learner Licence and Permanent Licence

A Learner Licence is generally the first stage for a person learning to drive before applying for the applicable permanent Driving Licence.

After meeting the applicable requirements and successfully completing the required driving test, an eligible applicant may receive the permanent Driving Licence.

Important: Requirements, waiting periods, tests and procedures can depend on the vehicle category and applicable government rules. Always check the latest official information.

Who can apply for a Driving Licence?

Eligibility depends on factors such as age, vehicle category and other applicable requirements.

Different vehicle categories can have different eligibility conditions. Check the current rules before applying.

Documents generally required

The exact documents can vary depending on the service and applicant. Common information or documents may include:

  • Proof of identity
  • Proof of address
  • Date of birth information
  • Photograph or digital photograph where applicable
  • Existing licence or learner details, where applicable
  • Medical or other supporting documents where required
Remember: Do not rely on an old document list. Check the latest requirements on the official transport portal for your application.

How to Apply for a Learner Licence?

  1. Open the authorised government transport portal.
  2. Select the appropriate learner licence service.
  3. Enter your personal details carefully.
  4. Provide the required documents and information.
  5. Complete the required application steps.
  6. Pay the applicable official fee, if required.
  7. Complete the required test or verification.
  8. Follow the instructions provided by the transport authority.

How to Apply for a Permanent Driving Licence?

  1. Meet the applicable learner licence requirements.
  2. Complete the required learning period or other applicable condition.
  3. Apply for the permanent Driving Licence through the authorised system.
  4. Submit the required information and documents.
  5. Book the driving test where required.
  6. Attend the test at the designated location.
  7. Complete the required verification process.

What happens during a Driving Test?

A driving test is designed to assess whether the applicant can safely operate the applicable vehicle according to the rules.

The exact test format can vary according to the vehicle category and applicable transport authority procedures.

Tip: Practise basic vehicle control, traffic signs, safe turns, stopping, starting and other skills before attending the test.

Driving Licence Renewal

A Driving Licence may need to be renewed according to its validity and applicable rules.

Renewal procedures, documents and fees can vary depending on the licence holder and applicable category.

Use the authorised transport service to check your eligibility and current renewal process.

Duplicate Driving Licence

If your Driving Licence is lost, damaged or otherwise needs replacement, an applicable duplicate or replacement service may be available.

The exact procedure depends on the reason for replacement and the applicable government process.

Change of Address or Other Details

If your address or other licence information changes, you may need to use the applicable update service.

Keep your licence information accurate and follow the official process for changes.

Digital Driving Licence

Government-authorised digital document services may allow eligible users to access digital versions of certain documents.

Use official government applications or services and keep your digital documents secure.

Driving Licence Safety Tips

  • Do not give your original licence to unknown people.
  • Do not upload licence documents to suspicious websites.
  • Use official transport portals for applications.
  • Do not share OTPs with anyone.
  • Be careful with fake agents promising guaranteed licences.
  • Keep your personal details secure.
  • Follow traffic laws even when carrying a valid licence.

Common Driving Licence Scams

Fraudsters may offer to arrange a Driving Licence without a test, promise instant approval or ask for money through unknown payment accounts.

Do not provide your OTP, password, banking PIN or other sensitive information to unknown people.

Stay safe: Always use authorised government services for Driving Licence applications and updates.
