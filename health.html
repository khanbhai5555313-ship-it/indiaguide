Build Healthy Everyday Habits

  • Stay physically active according to your abilities.
  • Choose a balanced and varied diet.
  • Get adequate sleep and maintain a regular routine.
  • Drink enough fluids throughout the day.
  • Keep regular medical checkups when appropriate.
  • Seek qualified medical advice when you have health concerns.
Important Health Notice: IndiaGuide provides general educational information and is not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis or treatment. For symptoms, emergencies, medicines or medical decisions, consult a qualified healthcare professional.
