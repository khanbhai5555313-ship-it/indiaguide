Loans in India: A Simple Guide

Learn about different types of loans in India, basic eligibility, documents, EMI, interest and important safety tips before borrowing money.

What Is a Loan?

A loan is money borrowed from a bank, financial institution or other lender that is repaid according to agreed terms.

The borrower normally repays the amount over a specific period. Depending on the loan, interest and other charges may apply.

Important: Loan interest rates, fees, eligibility requirements and approval conditions vary between lenders and can change over time.

Types of Loans in India

💳 Personal Loan

An unsecured loan generally used for personal financial needs. A lender may assess income, credit history and repayment capacity.

🏠 Home Loan

A loan used for eligible housing-related purposes such as buying, building or improving a property, subject to lender conditions.

🚗 Vehicle Loan

Financing for purchasing an eligible car, motorcycle or other vehicle. The vehicle may be used as security depending on the loan arrangement.

🎓 Education Loan

Financing intended for eligible education expenses such as tuition, depending on the lender's rules and the course or institution.

🏢 Business Loan

Financing for eligible business requirements such as working capital or expansion, subject to lender assessment.

👨‍👩‍👧 Other Loans

Lenders may also offer loans for specific purposes. Always check the exact terms before accepting any offer.

Secured vs Unsecured Loans

Secured Loan

A secured loan is generally backed by an asset or other acceptable security. Examples can include certain home or vehicle loans.

Unsecured Loan

An unsecured loan generally does not require the borrower to provide a specific asset as security. Personal loans are a common example.

The exact security requirements depend on the lender and the specific loan product.

Basic Loan Eligibility

There is no single eligibility rule for every loan. Lenders may consider:

  • Age and legal eligibility
  • Income and employment or business profile
  • Credit history
  • Existing financial obligations
  • Repayment capacity
  • Loan amount requested
  • Purpose of the loan
  • Security or collateral, where applicable

Meeting basic criteria does not guarantee loan approval. The final decision depends on the lender's assessment.

Documents Commonly Needed

Depending on the lender and type of loan, you may be asked for:

  • Identity proof
  • Address proof
  • PAN or other applicable tax identification details
  • Income or salary documents
  • Bank account statements
  • Employment or business information
  • Photographs
  • Property or vehicle documents, where applicable
Document requirements can differ by lender, customer profile and loan type. Always check the lender's official requirements.

What Is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the regular payment made toward a loan according to the repayment schedule.

An EMI can include both the principal amount and interest component. The amount and repayment structure depend on factors such as the loan amount, interest rate and tenure.

Loan Interest Rate

Interest is the cost of borrowing money. Different lenders and loan products may use different interest-rate structures.

Before accepting a loan, understand whether the quoted rate is fixed, floating or otherwise structured according to the product terms.

Don't look only at the interest rate. Check the total repayment amount, processing charges, penalties, insurance or other applicable costs and the complete loan agreement.

Steps to Apply for a Loan

  1. Decide why you need the loan.
  2. Estimate how much you can comfortably repay.
  3. Compare suitable loan products.
  4. Check the lender's eligibility criteria.
  5. Prepare the required documents.
  6. Submit the application through an official channel.
  7. Read the loan terms carefully before accepting.
  8. Keep copies of important documents and communications.

Loan Safety Tips

  • Use the lender's official website or verified branch/channel.
  • Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords with callers.
  • Do not pay unknown people promising guaranteed loan approval.
  • Read the complete loan agreement before accepting.
  • Check all fees and repayment conditions.
  • Be careful with loan apps and links received through messages.
  • Do not borrow more than you can reasonably repay.
Beware of loan scams. Fraudsters may promise instant approval or very low rates and then ask for personal information or upfront payments.

What If You Miss an EMI?

If you think you may miss a repayment, contact your lender through an official channel as soon as possible.

Late or missed payments can have financial consequences and may affect your credit history depending on the circumstances and reporting rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a loan?

A loan is money borrowed from a lender and repaid according to agreed terms, generally with interest and applicable charges.

Which documents are required for a loan?

Requirements vary. Identity and address proof, income documents, bank statements and purpose-related documents may be requested.

What does EMI mean?

EMI means Equated Monthly Instalment, a regular payment made toward a loan according to its repayment schedule.

Does getting a loan depend on credit history?

Credit history can be one factor considered by lenders, along with income, existing obligations and other eligibility criteria.

Should I compare loans before applying?

Yes. Compare the interest rate, fees, tenure, repayment conditions and overall cost before choosing a loan.
